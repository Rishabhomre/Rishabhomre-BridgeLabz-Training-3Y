The Art of Clear Typography

How type choices make a message easier to read.

Good typography gives words a comfortable rhythm and helps readers understand what matters most.

Make every word work

Headings can use a strong weight to create hierarchy, while body copy benefits from a generous line height. A little emphasis can guide the reader without interrupting the flow.

Use highlighted phrases sparingly to draw attention to important ideas. Keep line length balanced, choose clear font families, and leave enough space around each paragraph.

“Clarity is a design choice.”

The goal is not decoration alone—it is communication that feels natural and effortless.
